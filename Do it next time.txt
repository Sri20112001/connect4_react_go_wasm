### Step 3 — Check one particularly important thing

Your fork implementation needs to count **real winning threats**, not merely "moves that look like they create two threats."

For a candidate move:

```text
board
  ↓
play candidate
  ↓
find all columns where player can immediately win
  ↓
count UNIQUE winning columns
```

Then:

```ts
winningColumns.length >= 2
```

means it's a fork.

This distinction matters because two winning cells in the **same column** aren't two independent threats. The opponent only needs to block that column once.
